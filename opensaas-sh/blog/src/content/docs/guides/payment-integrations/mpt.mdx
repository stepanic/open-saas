First, make sure you've defined your payment processor in `src/payment/paymentProcessor.ts`, as described in the [important first steps](/guides/payment-integrations/).

[MPT](https://mpt.hr) lets your users pay with a SEPA bank transfer. They scan a QR code (EPC or Croatian HUB3) in their banking app, and the euros arrive at your [Safe](https://safe.global) as EURe, an e-money token issued by [Monerium](https://monerium.com), a regulated e-money institution. MPT tells your app about the payment with a signed webhook.

:::caution[One-time payments only]
A bank transfer is a one-time push payment, so MPT can't renew a subscription. Use it for plans with a `credits` effect (e.g. `Credits10`). Checking out a subscription plan (`Hobby`, `Pro`) throws an error.
:::

### Sandbox and Production

MPT runs on top of Monerium, which has two fully separate environments:
- Sandbox (`api.monerium.dev`): test SEPA transfers, no real money.
- Production (`api.monerium.app`).

On the MPT side, **your key decides the environment.** A sandbox key pays through Monerium's sandbox, a production key through production. There is no separate "sandbox mode" flag: to go live you swap the key, the webhook secret and the Safe address.

### Get your MPT credentials

You need four values for your `.env.server` file:

```ini title=".env.server"
MPT_API_URL=https://mpt.domovina.ai
MPT_SECRET_KEY=sk_...
MPT_WEBHOOK_SECRET=whsec_...
MPT_TARGET_ADDRESS=0x...
```

- `MPT_SECRET_KEY`: your tenant's secret key. Keep it on the server only.
- `MPT_WEBHOOK_SECRET`: the secret MPT signs webhooks with.
- `MPT_TARGET_ADDRESS`: the Safe that receives the EURe. It must be on your tenant's payout whitelist.

Contact MPT through [mpt.hr](https://mpt.hr) for a sandbox tenant, and tell them your webhook URL (see below).

### Set your plan prices

MPT has no product catalog: a payment is just an amount. So for MPT, each `PAYMENTS_*_PLAN_ID` env var holds the plan's **price in EUR**:

```ini title=".env.server"
PAYMENTS_CREDITS_10_PLAN_ID=9.99
# Subscriptions are not supported by MPT, but the env vars are still required.
PAYMENTS_HOBBY_SUBSCRIPTION_PLAN_ID=0
PAYMENTS_PRO_SUBSCRIPTION_PLAN_ID=0
```

### Webhooks

MPT sends events to your server's `/payments-webhook` endpoint and signs them with the [Standard Webhooks](https://www.standardwebhooks.com/) scheme. Open SaaS verifies the signature and handles each event once:

| Event | What Open SaaS does |
|---|---|
| `intent.paid` | Adds the plan's credits to the user. |
| `payment.late` | Same as `intent.paid`: the user paid in full after the payment window closed. |
| `payment.received` | Nothing yet. Monerium holds the funds and `intent.paid` follows, usually within seconds. |
| `payment.rejected`, `payment.underpaid` | Logs a warning and grants nothing. |

During development MPT has to reach your local server. Expose port `3001` with a tunnel such as [ngrok](https://ngrok.com/) and register `https://<your-tunnel>/payments-webhook` as your webhook URL with MPT.

In production, register `https://<your-server-url>/payments-webhook`.

### Test a payment

1. Start your app with `wasp start` and log in.
2. Go to the pricing page and buy the credits plan. You are redirected to the MPT checkout page with a QR code.
3. Make a test transfer in Monerium's sandbox to the IBAN and reference shown on the checkout page.
4. Watch your server logs: once `intent.paid` arrives, the user's credits go up.

### Deploying

When you deploy, set the production `MPT_SECRET_KEY`, `MPT_WEBHOOK_SECRET` and `MPT_TARGET_ADDRESS` on your server, and register your production webhook URL with MPT.
